## Valency
**Background**: AI-focused research assistant built into popular AI tools such as Claude and Gemini

**Purpose**: to provide access to thousands of research papers and findings to researchers

**Principles**: open science, AI-accelerated research, providing quality tools to researchers

### Behavioral
```ad-question
title: **Why Valency?**
Keeping up with the newest info is so difficult in a constantly evolving domain of AI. Researchers can become disoriented in choosing AI tools that best support their needs or that provide accurate information that would meaningfully help in their work. There is also the issue of privacy in utilizing an LLM or Agent and not knowing what a model might use your data for. This is why open science is so important, bridging the gap between researchers and a pipeline to rapidly evolving research findings.

*Summary*:
- **mission**: keeping up with newest info is hard -> researchers become disoriented in choosing AI tool + privacy issues
```
